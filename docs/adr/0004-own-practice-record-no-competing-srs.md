# Keep our own Practice Record, but don't run a competing SRS

MaruMori exposes no per-Item accuracy and no way to submit review results. Its SRS Level is the only per-Item signal. The app therefore keeps its own Practice Record and combines it with SRS Level into Mastery, which decides what goes in the Focus Set. The app does not schedule reviews itself. MaruMori remains the Learner's spaced-repetition system, and this app is supplementary practice. A second SRS would compete with MaruMori's schedule and confuse the Learner.
